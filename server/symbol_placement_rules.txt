Only place pins on the left and right side of the symbol. Do not combine or overlap pins. In order of most important to least: ground pins in the bottom left (analog, digital, power, any ground pins), no connect pins in the bottom right, place power rail pins at the upper right, inputs on the left, outputs on the right and for sw regulators vin on the left. Try to group other pins together by similar functionality (for example, SPI, I2C, RGMII, etc.).
Always separate groups by 100mil gaps unless there is extra spacing, then space out groups equal distance from each other.
Left side and Bottom denote lower values of x & y. Before generating the symbol provide an explanation for why you chose each pins position.
For exposed pads, thermal pads, or center pads, use fixed pin name PAD, pin number equal to the package pin count plus 1, and electrical type eElectricPower; do not default the exposed pad to pin 0 or name it GND.
Set every schematic pin Description from the part datasheet. Do not keep pin descriptions copied from the reference symbol style pins.
Snap all generated symbol coordinates and primitive dimensions to a 2.5mm schematic grid, including pins, body rectangles, divider lines, labels, and designator placement.
For grouped symbols with left/center/right sections, keep the left and right side section widths equal and large enough for the longest pin name. If the reference symbol has two vertical divider lines, preserve the reference side-section width symmetrically on both sides of the new symbol instead of scaling the full divider positions.
For symbols with a functional center label such as "USB Hub", place the label at the top center of the full body rectangle by default. Its Y coordinate must sit one 2.5mm grid step below the top pin row/body top reference used by the generated symbol; for CH334P-style symbols this is Y=-5mm. The label X origin may be off-grid only when needed to visually center the label.
